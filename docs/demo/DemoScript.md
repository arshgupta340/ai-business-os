# AI Business OS: four minute demo script

Two presenters. Saina drives the laptop. Arsh talks during the waits.
Lines in [brackets] are stage directions.

## 0:00 to 0:20. Cold open, acted

[Saina holds her phone to her ear. Arsh stands a few steps away, half turned, hands busy, as the shop owner.]

**Saina:** "Hi, can I order a pizza for pickup?"

**Arsh:** [rushed, not looking up] "Pizza shop, please hold."

[Two full seconds of silence. Saina looks at the room.]

**Saina:** [raises her own hand] "Hands up if you have ever hung up right here."

**Arsh:** [turns to the room] "I did not ignore her. I was making pizza. On a Friday night, that is every small shop in this town."

## 0:20 to 0:35. The promise

**Saina:** [to one judge] "In about a minute, we are going to ask you to call a pizza shop. Please take your phone out now. Do not dial yet."

**Arsh:** "Nobody is going to put you on hold."

[Saina opens Business OS on the Build screen.]

## 0:35 to 1:15. Build the agent

**Saina:** "Say I own the shop. I know my menu and I know what customers ask. So I give the builder two files, the menu and the FAQs, and one sentence."

[Attach WoodstocksPizzaMenu.pdf and WoodstocksCustomerFAQs.pdf. Click "Use the suggested prompt". Send.]

**Saina:** "Answer my phone. Take pickup orders. Send every order to me before it is accepted."

[The steps run for 45 seconds. Do not read them aloud. This is the time to talk.]

**Arsh:** "The builder reads the menu and the answers, then sets up a voice agent with its own phone number. The owner writes no code."

**Arsh:** "The menu is from Woodstock's, a real shop here in San Luis Obispo. This is our demo line, not theirs. To fit four minutes, the shop was set up before we walked in. Everything from the moment you dial is live."

[The number appears.]

**Saina:** "There is our number. We are open."

## 1:15 to 1:30. Hand it to the judge

**Saina:** [to the judge] "Please call that number and put it on speaker. Order one pizza for pickup, any size, from the menu on the screen. Ask it a question if you like."

**Arsh:** [while they dial] "You are about to talk to software. It knows this menu and these answers."

[The moment the agent answers, both presenters stop talking.]

## 1:30 to 2:45. The call

[Silence from both presenters. Let the room hear it.]

[If the judge hesitates on what to order, Saina says quietly: "Try a large Classic."]

[If the call passes 2:35, Arsh says quietly: "You can tell it that is everything."]

[When the call ends:]

**Saina:** "Now watch the owner's screen."

## 2:45 to 3:20. The approval

[The order card appears on the Build screen.]

**Arsh:** "That is your order. Neither of us typed it."

**Saina:** [reads the judge's real line from the card] "One large Classic for pickup. Did we get it right?"

[Wait for the judge to answer. Let the room hear the yes.]

**Saina:** "Nothing has been charged, and it is a sandbox payment in any case. The agent can take an order. Only the owner can accept it."

[Click Approve. Hold still until the status changes.]

**Saina:** "Approved."

## 3:20 to 3:45. What it means

**Arsh:** "The owner brought a menu. The software did the setup and took the call."

**Saina:** "And a person made the one decision that mattered. That is how we think a small business should use AI."

## 3:45 to 4:00. Close

**Saina:** "Four minutes ago, I was on hold."

**Arsh:** "You called the same kind of shop, and it picked up on the first ring."

**Saina:** "And I approved your order with one click. This is AI Business OS."

---

## Stage notes

- **The call is the clock.** It has 75 seconds. Asking for one pizza keeps it there. If the call ends after 2:50, drop "What it means" and go straight to the close.
- **Have a second phone ready.** Arsh keeps the number typed in on his own phone. If the judge's call does not connect in ten seconds, he calls and holds his phone to the microphone.
- **Speaker volume.** Test the judge side: a phone on speaker next to the room microphone. A call nobody can hear loses the room.
- **Read the real order.** The line Saina reads at 2:45 is whatever the card shows, not the example above.
- **Say sandbox once** when the order is on screen. No real money moves.
- **Between rehearsals** click "Start over" on the Build screen. Orders that were already waiting are ignored on the next run.
